## [0.1.0-prealpha] - unreleased

> Planned content of milestone 1; final once neVs accepts the slice.

### Added

- The complete escape route through five zones: Arrival, Maintenance, Supply, Security and Airlock.
- Six robot opponents: guard, shield bearer, sentinel, dog, kamikaze drone and the overseer boss with two phases.
- Improvised weapons: a torn-off robot arm, pistol, smg, scattergun, energy rifle, shield and throwable crates.
- Weapon-bench mods to upgrade guns with shards.
- Checkpoints for saving and respawning, and a death cache that lets Rend recover his shards once.
- A quick-maths pressure door after the relay checkpoint, announced by sound and light with a visible countdown.
- An intro comic of up to six panels, skippable after the first start.
- Accessibility options: subtitles for announcements, and slow motion and screen shake that can each be switched off; alarm lights flash at most 3 Hz.
- All in-game text in English from a single text table.
