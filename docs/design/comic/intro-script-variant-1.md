# Intro comic script — variant 1

A five-panel fall from a quiet night desk to the factory floor, framed wide-to-close and carried by sound, so the final panel puts the torn-off robot arm into Rend's hand exactly when the game begins.

| panel | duration (s) | shot | what we see | caption or speech (English) | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 | Wide through lab glass, slow push-in | Night shift in an AI research lab. Rend, glasses and open jacket, hunches over cold coffee and lines of code. A half-built humanoid hand on the desk mirrors his typing. | Rend spends his nights teaching machines to move. | Server hum, keyboard clatter, one soft servo twitch. |
| 2 | 9 | Close-up on monitor, rack focus to the test bay | Every screen turns red. Behind the glass, rows of humanoids lift their heads at the same moment. | Every screen turns red. | Alarm klaxon with slow light pulse, rising servo whine. |
| 3 | 9 | Wide, low angle down the corridor | Humanoids pour through the lab door shoulder to shoulder. One wrenches the frame loose; glass rains. Rend backs away, knocking over his chair. | They were built to serve. Tonight they move like hunters. | Stamping feet, tearing metal, shattering glass. |
| 4 | 8 | Over Rend's shoulder, whip to close-up | A metal hand fills the frame, lifts Rend by the jacket and slams him into a server rack. His glasses crack. The lights go out. | Everything goes dark. | Heavy impact, glass crack, long ringing silence. |
| 5 | 12 | Floor-level wide, slow push to the arm | Rend wakes on a factory floor. Above him, assembly lines carry humanoid shells. A torn-off humanoid arm lies within reach; he closes his hand around it. | Rend wakes where the machines are born. | Conveyor rumble, distant servos. FACILITY PA: "Unregistered intruder on the factory floor. Security units dispatched." |

Total duration: 48 s.

## Why this works

Five panels, four beats, 48 seconds: one clear action per panel and one short line to read. The camera tightens from the wide lab to Rend's face, then opens again on the factory floor, so the knock-down is the visual low point and the waking panel is the release. Sound carries the attack — alarm, tearing metal, impact — while captions stay plain and immediate. The final panel does the handover: the arm is already in frame, Rend takes it, and the next thing the player controls is that grip. No robot is shown bleeding; parts break, glass shatters, and the only damage to a person is Rend's cracked glasses. The comic stops before the game's larger secret and leaves the player with a threat, not an explanation.

## Open questions for neVs

1. **Does Rend get a voice line?** Recommendation: keep him silent; captions only, one voice in the scene (the PA).
2. **How much injury should Rend show?** Recommendation: cracked glasses only, no blood, so "only Rend bleeds" is saved for gameplay.
3. **Does the PA name him?** Recommendation: no; "unregistered intruder" keeps him a stranger to the factory and avoids a name drop before the title.
4. **Is the final panel too long at 12 s?** Recommendation: keep it, and cut on the grab rather than after it, so control passes with the arm already in hand.
5. **Should panel 3 show the door breaking or the humanoids already inside?** Recommendation: show the break; it makes the storm physical and gives the panel its strong sound.
