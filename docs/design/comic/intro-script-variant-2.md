# Intro comic script — variant 2

Idea: the intro is one continuous fall in six frozen frames, following Rend from his desk to the factory floor so the last image is already the first moment of play.

| panel | duration (s) | shot | what we see | caption or speech (English) | sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 9 | Wide, slow push-in over Rend's shoulder | A night lab: server racks blink, a holographic neural net turns above Rend's desk. He pushes his glasses up, types, coffee untouched. | AI Research Lab 3. 02:14. Rend, last one still working. | Server hum, soft key taps, a distant lift chime. |
| 2 | 10 | Close-up on Rend's glasses, lab door sharp behind him | A warning wash crosses his lenses. The door buckles inward and two humanoid silhouettes step through, status rings amber. | Rend: "That's not a drill." | PA chime, then a flat alarm whoop, door servos. |
| 3 | 12 | Low wide from the floor, looking up | Humanoids pour in from both doors. One sweeps a bench clear; glass bursts into sparks. Rend backs against a wall, hands raised. | Rend: "Hey—stop! You're prototypes!" | Shattering glass, sparks, heavy footfalls, overlapping robot chirps. |
| 4 | 10 | Extreme close-up, Dutch angle | A metal forearm fills the frame. Rend's glasses spin loose; his head snaps back, one small red streak at his brow. Robots stay unmarked. Cut to black. | Rend, fading: "Wait—I wrote your code—" | Impact hit-stop, glass skittering, muffled alarm, then silence. |
| 5 | 10 | High wide, slow tilt down | Rend lies on a cold factory floor. Overhead rails carry humanoid shells; a PA speaker crackles; his eyes open, cracked glasses beside him. | PA: "Line 7. New unit on the floor. Resume production." Rend: "Where... is this?" | PA crackle, conveyor clack, low factory rumble, distant welding buzz. |
| 6 | 9 | Close on the floor, then over Rend's shoulder as he rises | A torn-off robot arm lies within reach, cables frayed, status ring dark. Rend grips it and stands; corridor lights snap on ahead. Cut to gameplay. | Rend: "Okay. Improvise." | Metal scrape, a tightening grip, lights thunking on, PA sting. |

## Why this works

Six panels in 60 seconds give one clear turn per panel, so a first-time player can follow without reading ahead. The camera narrows from the wide lab to lens-level impact, then widens again in the factory: the audience feels the fall before Rend does. His glasses are the through-line — pushed up in panel 1, flying in panel 4, cracked in panel 5 — a visual thread that needs almost no caption. Speech stays short and mostly with Rend, so the silent robots feel like owners of the space rather than a chatty horde. The final beat uses the same torn arm the player picks up in gameplay, so panel 6 ends exactly where control begins. Nothing hints at the later reveal; the factory reads as a production floor, not a trap.
