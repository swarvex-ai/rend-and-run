# Story outline

## Premise

Rend is a nerd who works in AI research; one day humanoids storm his lab, knock him down and he wakes in a factory of humanoids (vision: Geschichte). He has to escape that factory with whatever he finds (vision: Geschichte). Only Rend bleeds; the machines break into parts (vision: Gegner).

## World rules

- The factory is a smooth, modern lab, with server hum and blinking servers behind some doors (vision: Welt und Klang).
- Its population is made of humanoids, drones and other robots that want to stop Rend, sometimes to capture him and sometimes to kill him (vision: Gegner).
- In M1 the robots only kill; capture is not part of v1 (brief: S18).
- Humanoids can use every weapon a person can use and carry all kinds of equipment (vision: Gegner).
- Drones carry explosives, including small mini-drones in swarms; other robots include wheeled and four-legged, doglike machines (vision: Gegner).
- Only Rend bleeds; a damaged robot breaks into parts and never shows blood (vision: Gegner).
- Rend's first weapon is the arm of a humanoid he finds, and every better weapon comes out of the factory itself (vision: Waffen und Ausrüstung).
- The factory reacts under pressure with alarms, reinforcements and announcements, so standing still is dangerous (brief: G4).
- The robots are original factory equipment, never named real products or people (brief: S13).
- The story is never told through a dialogue system; it reaches the player through comics, PA announcements and the environment (brief: G5).

## Characters

### Rend

- **Role:** the hero, an AI researcher who wakes in the humanoid factory and has to escape it (vision: Geschichte).
- **Look:** a nerd, soft and unarmoured, the only human in a factory built for machines (vision: Geschichte).
- **Speaks:** silence; with no dialogue system, his story is carried by comics, PA announcements and the environment (brief: G5).
- **Vision line:** "Rend ist ein Nerd und arbeitet in der KI-Forschung ... wacht in einer Fabrik der Humanoiden wieder auf" (vision: Geschichte).

### Guard

- **Role:** the first and most common pressure, a light humanoid that works in squads (brief: S13).
- **Look:** a clean-limbed factory humanoid, built to handle the same weapons a person can (vision: Gegner).
- **Speaks:** short tactical lines through a body speaker, never a conversation (new — needs neVs).
- **Vision line:** "Humanoide in unterschiedlichen Designs ... Sie können alle Waffen benutzen, die auch ein Mensch benutzen kann" (vision: Gegner).

### Shield bearer

- **Role:** a humanoid that blocks Rend's way with a riot shield and punishes careless frontal hits (brief: S13).
- **Look:** a humanoid behind a broad shield, a moving wall rather than a duelist (brief: S13).
- **Speaks:** silence, the clang of the shield and the sound of servos (vision: Gegner).
- **Vision line:** "Humanoide in unterschiedlichen Designs ... setzen allerlei Ausrüstung ein" (vision: Gegner).

### Sentinel

- **Role:** an ambusher that waits in a wall alcove and wakes when Rend runs past (brief: S13).
- **Look:** a narrow humanoid half-hidden in the wall, its head ring the only bright part (new — needs neVs).
- **Speaks:** silence until it wakes, then light and servo noise instead of words (vision: Gegner).
- **Vision line:** "Humanoide in unterschiedlichen Designs" (vision: Gegner).

### Dog

- **Role:** a four-legged pursuer that flanks Rend and pressures him at ground level (brief: S13).
- **Look:** a low, horizontal doglike robot, all angles and legs (vision: Gegner).
- **Speaks:** servo growls and the clatter of feet, with no words at all (new — needs neVs).
- **Vision line:** "Weitere Roboter, zum Beispiel fahrende und vierbeinige, hundeartige" (vision: Gegner).

### Kamikaze drone

- **Role:** a flying one-shot threat that dives at Rend and explodes (brief: S13).
- **Look:** a small rotor drone carrying an explosive charge instead of tools (vision: Gegner).
- **Speaks:** a rising motor whine, then the blast (vision: Gegner).
- **Vision line:** "Drohnen, mit Sprengstoff bestückt, auch kleine Mini-Drohnen im Schwarm" (vision: Gegner).

### Overseer

- **Role:** the boss that supervises the lab, guards the airlock and intercepts Rend (brief: S21).
- **Look:** a heavy humanoid built on the brute chassis, broad and armoured (brief: S13).
- **Speaks:** the sector's calm PA voice, then silence when it steps out to fight (new — needs neVs).
- **Vision line:** no direct line; it is extrapolated from "Humanoide in unterschiedlichen Designs" (vision: Gegner).

## Beats per milestone

### M1

1. **Intro comic:** a night in Rend's AI lab, the humanoid raid, the knock-down and his awakening on the factory floor; at most six panels and sixty seconds, unskippable on the first start (vision: Comic).
2. **Wake-up:** Rend rises among assembly lines, finds a torn-off humanoid arm as his first weapon and faces the first guard (vision: Waffen und Ausrüstung).
3. **Arrival:** the wake-up room teaches movement, taking a weapon from the world and fighting back (brief: S13).
4. **Maintenance:** a service sector with a shortcut door and a power section; the PA announces a lockdown (brief: S8).
5. **Supply:** Rend takes the shield, activates the first checkpoint and reaches the workbench (brief: S14).
6. **Security:** shield bearer, gun guards, dog, sentinels and kamikaze drones apply the pressure (brief: S13).
7. **Security, later half:** the relay checkpoint sits before a pressure-task door where a wrong answer kills Rend (brief: S16).
8. **Overseer:** in the airlock arena the boss fights in two phases and announces every strike at least half a second ahead (brief: S21).
9. **Airlock:** with the overseer destroyed the security door opens and Rend escapes through the airlock (vision: Geschichte).

## Channels

- **Comic:** the intro and later in-game inserts tell the story in one fixed style; the intro runs before the first start and cannot be skipped on that first run (vision: Comic).
- **PA announcements:** the factory voice carries airlock, assembly, capture, escape, intact and lockdown lines at fixed route cues, with subtitles for accessibility (brief: S8).
- **Environment:** the smooth modern lab, server hum, blinking servers, traps and route dressing carry story without words (vision: Welt und Klang).
- **Not a channel:** there is no dialogue system; comics, PA announcements and the environment are the only narration channels (brief: G5).

## Later (not in M1)

- **Spoiler:** later the game reveals that sadistic humanoids capture the remaining humans and make them run a parcours; the data feeds the robots' AI so the next round catches and kills better (vision: Geschichte).
- Later, capture becomes a real outcome instead of only killing (brief: S18).
- Later comic inserts continue the story in the same style and still cannot be skipped away (vision: Comic).
- Later the mini-drone swarm, wheeled robots and the farming tree widen the hunt (brief: S13).

## Open questions for neVs

1. **Does Rend stay silent?** The intro scripts disagree: one keeps him voiceless, one gives him lines. Recommendation: keep him silent in M1 and let captions and the PA carry the story.
2. **Do robots get spoken lines in M1?** Recommendation: allow short tactical lines with subtitles, because the audio already exists, but never a conversation.
3. **How much of the capture protocol may the PA hint at in M1?** Recommendation: keep the "returned intact" and "capture protocol" lines as cold procedure, without explaining the later reveal.
4. **Which comic variant becomes the reference?** Recommendation: pick the variant whose Rend is silent, so the comic and the no-dialogue rule agree.
5. **Is the overseer's PA voice the same voice as the factory PA?** Recommendation: yes, so the boss sounds like the building itself.
